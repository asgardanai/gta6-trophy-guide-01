Checklist Roadmap to Platinum Missables 7

The road to the platinum in four stages, built from the official trophy descriptions. Story first, then strangers, then the map, then the grinds.

Seven trophies carry a missable flag. The flags come from the official descriptions alone — the game is a day old, so nothing here is confirmed in-game. Yes means the description points at a real one-way window; possible means it might be one, and the note says why.

Sources. Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles (18 November 2026), the two agreeing on every name, description and grade; PSN counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas, 9 gangs, 8 garages, 9 activity types, 500 choices) are PSNProfiles' progress targets. Every guide is written for this entry, in our own words, from the official description — and where a step is not pinned down in any published source, the guide says so instead of inventing it. Links open only if you follow them; the app itself fetches nothing and works with the network off.
